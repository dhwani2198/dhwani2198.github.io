How might we streamline expense tracking and organization for freelancers and startups so it requires minimal time and effort?

Tally began as an exploration into making financial tracking feel less like traditional bookkeeping and more like a seamless part of daily workflows. Instead of building a full accounting platform, it focuses on a lightweight system that enables independent professionals to quickly capture expenses, organize them by project, and maintain real-time visibility into spending.

By automating expense tracking for freelancers and small businesses, Tally eliminates manual data entry. Users can simply upload a receipt image or send a message, and AI extracts, organizes, and securely records the expense in real time.
