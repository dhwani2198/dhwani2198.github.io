Sprint X reimagines Agile execution by embedding AI into the core workflow, automatically breaking down complex tasks, generating data driven sprint retrospectives, and eliminating repetitive administrative work, enabling teams to focus on execution rather than process management.

SOLUTION

The core of SprintX is the Context-Rich Issue Detail view, primarily designed to eliminate tab-switching.


Instead of navigating across tools, specs in a doc, designs in Figma, and code in GitHub, users can access everything they need within a single view.
